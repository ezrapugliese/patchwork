# patchwork - a free, in browser, home for your plural system.

## what is this about?
- **patchwork** is an up and coming web app meant to house and organize members, switches, ideas, relationships, and more; developed by a system, for plurals and systems alike.
- patchwork is fully browser based, and no data is sent anywhere; its completely private. nobody, not even me, can access your data. 
- **version 1.0.0** expected by the end of november 2026.
- try the latest build here: [pw-dev.vercel.app](https://pw-dev.vercel.app). be aware this build is a **work in progress.** it may be buggy, unfinished, and data may be lost. use at your own risk!

## features
- **dashboard, system, alters, and settings pages** - the core layout of the app, navigable from the sidebar.
- **quick switcher (Ctrl/Cmd + K)** - pull up a fast alter switcher from anywhere in the app.
- **activity log with undo** - every change you make gets logged for the session, and most actions can be undone right from the log.
- **in-page search** - search the page you're currently on without leaving patchwork.
- **responsive layout** - collapsible sidebars for smaller screens.
